Full-stack booking marketplace

The web app that connects people to salons & local services—and runs the business behind the counter

YoyakuYo (Bookyo) is not a single feature. It’s a public discovery site, guest booking to reserve a slot, a LINE mini-app for on-the-go customers, a full owner back-office (calendar, services, messaging), and an admin console to keep listings fair and safe. Built with Next.js, Supabase, a Node API, and LINE.

Visit live sitehttps://www.yoyakuyo.jp/

Three sides of the same product

Same data and bookings—different screens for each role.

A

Customers (web)

Browse by category and area, open shop pages, pick a time, and book as a guest (no forced account). Leave reviews after a visit and reach shops via shop number on flyers or deep links.

L

Customers (LINE)

A LINE mini-app (LIFF) mirrors key flows: home, search, favorites, bookings, inbox / messages, chat, booking inside LINE, and support—so repeat visits happen where people already chat.

B

Business owners

Dashboard, calendar & availability, services & shop profile, photos, bookings & customers, messages, reviews, verification / claims, AI settings, and owner support.

Ops

Platform administrators

Separate admin area: shops & verified shops, shop claims, users, bookings, disputes, reviews, analytics, notifications, and support—so the marketplace stays trustworthy at scale.

Customer journey on the website

From first visit to a confirmed reservation—typical paths built into the app.

1

Discover

Marketing landing (hero, categories, reviews, LINE QR). Browse by area (region / prefecture / city), categories, featured & trending shops, shop lists and detail pages—including maps where relevant.

2

Choose & book

Pick services and time; guest booking flows for people who don’t want to sign up. Optional paths: reschedule flows where the product supports them.

3

Confirm & show up

The booking is confirmed in the app. Customers track guest bookings, manage reschedules where supported, and can leave reviews after the visit.

+

Alternate entry: shop number

Owners get a short shop number (and verification code) for posters. Customers type the number on web or in LINE to jump straight to that shop—one of several ways to arrive, alongside browse and search.

What owners run day to day

  • · Operations: live calendar, availability rules, incoming bookings, customer list.
  • · Catalog: services, durations, gallery photos, public shop profile.
  • · Growth & trust: reviews, verification, claiming a listing.
  • · Communication: messaging with customers; AI-related settings where enabled.

How it’s built

  • WEB Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind, next-intl for multiple languages on the customer-facing site.
  • DATA Supabase (Postgres, auth patterns, RLS-oriented design) plus SQL migrations for schema evolution.
  • API Node backend for shops, bookings, LINE-related behavior, and other domain logic.
  • LINE LIFF / LINE surfaces so the product lives inside Japan’s dominant chat app.
